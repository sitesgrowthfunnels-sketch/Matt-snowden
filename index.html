Companies & Work

Matthew Snowden is the founder and CEO of Ten Sixty AI, an artificial intelligence platform focused on the future of real estate and institutional decision-making.

Previously, he founded Anti-Hero Capital, one of Australia’s earliest bitcoin venture funds, built around ideas from evolutionary systems and complexity science. His work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, markets, cognition, and emerging technologies.

Matthew created and produced the globally successful Walking with Dinosaurs app and games, developed in collaboration with BBC Earth and Stephen Fry, which became the number one app worldwide and was featured by Apple.

Over the past decade, he has advised startups across AI, crypto, gaming, and digital infrastructure, including Enjin Coin, a leading blockchain gaming company, and Harold AI, a leading commercial real estate AI platform. He has collaborated with founders, creators, and public figures across technology, media, and finance, including Stephen Fry, Jason Oppenheim (“Selling Sunset”, Netflix) and Robert Kiyosaki (Rich Dad, Poor Dad).

Matthew’s companies and products have been featured in publications and media outlets including The New York Times, the Today Show, and dozens of international news sites.

In his upcoming book “Adaptive Velocity”, he draws on a career spanning artificial intelligence, venture capital, behavioural systems, and technological disruption to explore how AI is reshaping organizations.
